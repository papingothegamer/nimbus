# Audit July 25, 2026

## Completed Tasks

- **Audio Engine & Clip Playback Fixes:**
  - Resolved a critical bug where duplicated audio clips (e.g., one-shots) failed to output sound when played back on the timeline.
  - Fixed an initialization flaw in the `DiskStreamer` where background streaming would begin buffering immediately at the audio file's start, causing a buffer underrun and forced seek (resulting in silence) when the playhead finally caught up to the duplicated clip's position.
  - Modified `AudioClipNode::prepareToPlay` to explicitly call `requestSeek()` to the clip's specific offset, ensuring that the background `DiskStreamer` buffer aligns precisely with the audio engine's render expectations when the clip starts playing.
  - Addressed a secondary edge case in `AudioClipNode::processBlock` to ensure the disk streamer correctly resets its position if the transport jumps to a location before the clip.
  - Updated the `AudioClip::clone()` method to correctly copy all audio properties (such as gain, pan, pitch shift, speed multiplier, reverse state, and fade curves) when a clip is duplicated or pasted, which were previously being omitted.

- **Fade Engine & UI Interaction:**
  - Implemented robust hit-testing and dragging mechanisms for fade handles, resolving issues where handles were difficult to target.
  - Added a visual header divider line and refined clip colors to ensure high contrast and clear distinction between the header and waveform areas.
  - Fixed a critical drag cancellation bug where `notifyClipModified()` was being called every frame during a mouse drag, forcing the entire `ClipComponent` to be destroyed and recreated mid-drag.
  - Fixed an issue where dragging the fade curve handles up/down behaved inversely to user expectations.
  - Reset the default fade curve from a 0.5 (concave) value to 1.0 (linear) to prevent new clips from defaulting to an aggressive exponential fade shape.
